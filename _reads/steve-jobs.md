## Creativity meets technology

*Steve Jobs* by Walter Isaacson explores the life of Apple's cofounder through interviews with Jobs and people who knew or worked with him. The book considers both his product achievements and the difficult aspects of his personality and working relationships.

### Topics to explore

- Connecting design, technology, and the user experience.
- The role of product vision in building a company.
- The strengths and costs of perfectionism.
- Examining leadership through both accomplishments and its effects on people.

The biography offers material for reflection on innovation and character, rather than a simple formula for business success.
