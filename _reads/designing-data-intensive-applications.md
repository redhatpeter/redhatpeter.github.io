## Reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems

*Designing Data-Intensive Applications* by Martin Kleppmann looks beneath the terminology of modern data systems to explain how different approaches work and what their trade-offs mean for application design.

Rather than prescribing one tool for every problem, the book helps readers compare technologies for storing and processing data against the needs of their systems.

### Topics to explore

- What reliability, scalability, and maintainability mean in practice.
- Comparing data storage and processing approaches.
- Understanding distributed-system trade-offs instead of relying on buzzwords.
- Choosing technologies based on application requirements.
