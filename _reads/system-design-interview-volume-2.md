## An insider's guide to more design problems

*System Design Interview: An Insider's Guide, Volume 2* by Alex Xu and Sahn Lam continues the series with a new set of worked design problems.

The book uses a structured approach and detailed diagrams to explain how different systems fit together. Its description notes that familiarity with basic distributed-systems concepts is useful, but reading Volume 1 is not required.

### Topics to explore

- Location-based services, nearby-friend features, and mapping systems.
- Distributed message queues and metrics monitoring.
- Object storage, email services, and reservation systems.
- Payment systems, digital wallets, and stock exchanges.

These case studies offer opportunities to compare requirements and trade-offs across different domains, rather than treating every system design problem as the same exercise.
