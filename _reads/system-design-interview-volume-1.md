## An insider's guide

*System Design Interview: An Insider's Guide* by Alex Xu introduces a step-by-step framework for approaching system design questions. Worked examples and diagrams connect the framework to concrete architecture decisions.

### Topics to explore

- Estimating capacity and reasoning about growth from a small service to millions of users.
- Designing rate limiters, key-value stores, and distributed ID generators.
- Applying consistent hashing and other distributed-system building blocks.
- Exploring application designs such as URL shorteners, news feeds, chat, and file storage.

The examples provide a way to practice explaining a design and its trade-offs, rather than a single architecture to memorize for every situation.
