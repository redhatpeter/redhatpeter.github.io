## An iterative process for production-ready applications

*Designing Machine Learning Systems* by Chip Huyen examines the decisions involved in taking machine learning beyond an isolated model and into a working production system.

The book treats data, models, evaluation, infrastructure, and business goals as connected parts of the same system. A choice that improves one component can introduce trade-offs elsewhere, so the right approach depends on the application and its constraints.

### Topics to explore

- How data and business requirements influence the design of an ML system.
- How prediction strategies and infrastructure choices affect deployment.
- How teams approach retraining, changing data distributions, and consistency between training and inference.
- Why production decisions need to account for different stakeholders, not just model performance.
