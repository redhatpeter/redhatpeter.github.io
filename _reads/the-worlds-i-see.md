## Curiosity, exploration, and discovery at the dawn of AI

*The Worlds I See* by Fei-Fei Li combines a personal memoir with an account of scientific discovery. It follows her family's immigration experience, her path into research, and her work on ImageNet and computer vision.

The book connects advances in artificial intelligence with the human experiences behind them, while considering the possibilities and responsibilities that accompany the technology.

### Topics to explore

- Curiosity and persistence in a scientific career.
- The relationship between personal circumstances and opportunity.
- ImageNet's place in the development of modern AI.
- Keeping human concerns central to technological progress.
