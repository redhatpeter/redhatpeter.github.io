## Amazon's 14 leadership principles

*The Amazon Way* by John Rossman examines the leadership principles and operating practices behind Amazon's business culture. Drawing on his experience as an Amazon executive, Rossman describes how broad principles can become everyday management habits.

### Topics to explore

- Starting with customer needs and working backward toward a solution.
- Using metrics to connect decisions with results.
- Simplifying processes as organizations grow.
- Distinguishing reversible decisions from decisions that require greater caution.

The title refers to the principles covered by this edition, rather than a statement about Amazon's current number of leadership principles.
