## Ambition, innovation, and leadership

*Elon Musk* by Walter Isaacson follows Musk's life and work, including his involvement with Tesla, SpaceX, and Twitter. Isaacson's account draws on time spent observing Musk and the people around him.

The biography examines technological ambition alongside the personal and organizational tensions that accompany an intense approach to leadership. It offers a subject for critical examination, not a management style to adopt without question.

### Topics to explore

- Risk-taking in technically demanding industries.
- The relationship between a strong mission and organizational pressure.
- How a leader's personality can influence teams and decisions.
- The trade-offs and consequences of pursuing ambitious goals.
