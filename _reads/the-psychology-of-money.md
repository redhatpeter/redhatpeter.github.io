## Timeless lessons on wealth, greed, and happiness

*The Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel looks at financial decisions through the lens of human behavior. Rather than treating money as a purely mathematical subject, the book explores how personal history, incentives, and emotions shape the choices people make.

### Topics to explore

- Why people with different experiences can approach money differently.
- How ego, expectations, and incentives influence financial behavior.
- The gap between knowing a financial concept and acting on it.
- Thinking about wealth and happiness beyond numerical measures.
